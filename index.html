Installation Guide

3 Steps to a Perfect Connection

No wire stripping. No special tools. Just a standard pair of pliers and 10 seconds per connection.

1

Place Wires Together

Hold the wires you want to splice side by side. No insulation stripping required prior to insertion.

2

Insert Into Connector

Push both wires together into the open end of the B-Connector until they reach the stop.

3

Crimp with Pliers

Use standard pliers to press the connector button flush. The IDC blade pierces insulation and grips the copper wire.

4

Test & Done

Give the wires a gentle tug to confirm the connection is secure. The job is complete.
